ShadowClaw Light Meter Workbench

Build a relative-light workbench using a TinyDuino, BLE112, and KY-018 photoresistor. Use ShadowClaw skills to interpret calibrated sensor readings—not to claim uncalibrated lux measurements.

Open the workbench guide or review hardware and project notes.

How it works

  1. Read the KY-018 ADC output with TinyDuino firmware.
  2. Send or copy a sample through the BLE112 integration you configure.
  3. Calibrate with dark and bright references, then ask ShadowClaw to interpret it.
